Our Sixth Date 🏌️

My first surprise date to you. You've been wanting to play golf ever since I mentioned it on our first date over ice cream. I didn't have my gold clubs yet so I figured the driving range would be a good alternative. I told the date would be a surprise and you just need to dress comfortably - you were so worried about this I had to give you hints and even approve the outfit 😂. I remember the morning of that day. I called ahead to Sportsman's Warehouse and made sure they had both men's and women's golf gloves before I picked you up and took you to eat at Circus Circus - lunch there was great btw! After I lied and said we needed to go to Sportsman's and I couldn't tell whether you were shocked, excited, or confused when I was buying us golf gloves. I explained myself after and your excitement couldn't be contained - you kissed me before we even got in the car! Literally didn't even let me open the door for you for something you - I knew then I did something special for you that day! At the driving range we barely paid attention to our shots - we were too busy laughing and trying to record each other. I just knew you were having a great time. You loved it so much you made me break my rule this time 🙃! Oh, and we got donuts after 🍩.
