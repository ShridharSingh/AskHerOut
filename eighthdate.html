Our Eigth Date 💅🏻

We supposed to have a picnic but somehow decided to get Panarotti's Pizza instead of our usual snacks. It was very busy that day and we couldn't get a place inside the resturant so we decided to take the pizza as our picnic meal. They took a while to make it so we walked to probably your favourite place in that mall, the book shop. You bought us coffee and we walked around the shop glancing at the books and flexing your reading catalog - it was proably at that moment I decided I wanted to start reading too. We then picked up our pizza and headed to Saunder's only to find out it was closed - again 🙃! And I thought another date was ruined, but luckily (or unluckily) we ended up at The Pearls again and we were prepping for a beach picnic but the weather was just not on our side and ended up eating in the car🙈. I felt so bad that we were having a date in the car and remember asking you a few times if you want to go somewhere else but you were happy just sitting in the backseat of a car eating and talking to me - I realised the you might actually love me. The date wasnt even over coz after we ate we took a stroll on the pier and along the promenade where we took some of my favourite pictures!
