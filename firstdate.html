Our First Date ❤️

I picked you up and instantly made you smile. You got in the car before I could open the door. We sat on the pier and I remember you were silently watching the waves whilst I was worried I didn't have enough to say. Little did I know you were starting to open up to me from then. Lunch at Grimaldi's was the first time we lost track of time and possibly the place I won you over. The walk to get ice cream after was a constant series of rejections for me to subltly hold your hand, but I finally stole my chance on the way back. We really did not want that day to end and extended the date at Mug 'n Bean, where we lost track of time again. Driving you home was a happy feeling and then you rejected my kiss🥲. But I respected that - you know we were not supposed to move too fast hey!👀
