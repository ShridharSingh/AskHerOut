Ask Her Out

Our Seventeenth Date 🚘

I bought tickets to Gas mototshow in March not knowing how long away it would actually be before we attended this show. It did not dissapoint! We arrived at Suncoast trying to find my friend - who ditched us shortly after. I was actually more comfortable with that to be honest, I did not want us getting crowded. We had a great time ourselves walking the event grounds and looking at the cars whilst making our own side comments about which car is better. You were only taking pictures of Porsches and BMW's and I was telling you about all the Japanese legends (yes that's what they're called). We took a lot of photos that day, but none of us as a couple, which, considering the environment, I did not mind at all! We waited in line at so many places that we got late for the official drifting event. When we finally arrived there was no space for us to watch them drifting and I thought the date was ruined. I don't know whether you sensed that I was a bit worried or if you were just having fun doing literally anything with me, but your response was simply to walk the food section and view the cars again. But I think it was meant to be because we ended up getting the best viewing spot for their unprompted drifting show and I just loved seeing how much you were enjoying. We met with my sister's cousins after and they were a lot of fun - I hope you were comfortable with them. Also we ended up eating crepes wrong (and I later found out it's pronounced 'CREAPE') and finally we sneakily took pictures of Kung Sang before we left for dinner at Atasca - which was amazing up until that homeless man came up to us👀.
