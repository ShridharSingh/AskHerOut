Ask Her Out

Our Second Date 💋

You asked me if I had a picnic blanket and I remember panicing slightly because I didn't have one. Luckily Kassie asked to meet that morning and we searched Gateway looking for picnic blankets. Kassie also showed me snacks to get for the picnic and as we were leaving we decided to get pillows for you and I to sit on. That date was special for you and I - you told me I didn't need to buy a picnic blanket and that you would've brought a hoodie for us. For me, that one simple sentence solidified that we both cared about each other enough to go out of our way for making the other comfortable. The date started at Fabrica and, like we usually do, we lost track of time talking 😂. It got so late that we had to forego buying snacks for our picnic and we just drove to Saunders. Turns out Saunders Park was closed and I thought the date was ruined. Thankfully we parked at The Pearls and had a lovely picnic on the beach. You watched the ships and told me about how it reminded you of your uncle who used to work on a ship. We watched your new favourite show 'The IT Crowd' and lay on our backs looking at the stars. The perfect moment was created so organically that I could not resist and broke the rules to kiss you for the first time. You seemed to enjoy it because you did not want to stop 🙃 - it literally took the rain to drive us out of there!
